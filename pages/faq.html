Are your peptides for human use?
No. All compounds distributed by Labsourced are strictly for in vitro and laboratory research use only. They are not intended for human or animal consumption.
What is the purity of your peptides?
Our peptides are synthesized and tested to ensure a purity of 99% or higher, validated through rigorous HPLC and MS analysis.
How long does shipping take?
Domestic orders typically arrive within 2-4 business days. International orders can take between 7-14 business days depending on customs processing in the destination country.
How do I track my order?
Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking code via email. You can enter this code on our "Track Order" page to see real-time updates.
