Once your order ships, you will receive your tracking code. You can track your order at any time on our Track Order page using your unique tracking code (format: LS-YYYYXXXXX).

International orders may be subject to customs fees, import duties, or taxes imposed by the destination country. These charges are the sole responsibility of the customer. Labsourced is not responsible for delays caused by customs processing.

Lost or Damaged Packages

If your package is lost or arrives damaged, please contact us within 48 hours at shipping@labsourced.co with your tracking code and photos of any damage. We will work to resolve the issue promptly.
